          About the Website

          The G12 Learning Materials Organizer is a web-based platform developed as part of a high school research project. It was designed to help students easily access, organize, and upload study materials for Grade 12 subjects in a single, intuitive interface.

          Unlike conventional storage tools such as Google Drive or Classroom, this platform focuses on academic organization, productivity, and engagement. It integrates features like an AI learning assistant, a built-in quiz section, and a Pomodoro timer to promote focused and self-paced study habits.

          About the Researchers

          This website was created by the student researchers of Grade 12 Lovelace of Tagbilaran City Science High School under the guidance of the research teachers, as part of their final research project in fulfillment of the requirements for Senior High School.
